# Stage 4 — Data Visualization, Results & Interpretation

## Purpose

Present the main analysis results through visualizations and interpret the patterns observed in the cleaned paper-leak dataset.

## Required checks

- Create a yearly view of recorded paper-leak incidents.
- Identify the top 10 areas with the most recorded incidents.
- Identify the top 10 exams with the most recorded incidents.
- Show the distribution of incidents by leak status.
- Compare recorded incidents across different eras.
- Visualize the relationship between recorded arrests and convictions.
- Interpret the main patterns shown by the visualizations.
- Keep the interpretation limited to what the dataset supports.

**Important:** The visualizations represent recorded incidents in the dataset and should not be interpreted as a complete count of every paper-leak incident in India. The 2026 data also represents only a partial year.

## Output

The stage produces the final charts and result interpretations used to communicate the findings of the project. The exported figures are stored in `outputs/figures/`.
